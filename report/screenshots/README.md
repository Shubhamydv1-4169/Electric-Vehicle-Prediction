🚀 Excited to share my latest End-to-End Data Science & Machine Learning Project: Electric Vehicle Prediction & Market Analytics! ⚡🚗📊

With the automotive industry accelerating toward complete electrification, understanding adoption dynamics, battery autonomy, and automaker positioning is crucial for manufacturers, utilities, and clean-energy policymakers.

In this project, I developed an end-to-end machine learning system using real-world Electric Vehicle Population data (Washington State DOL / Data.gov) to predict electric vehicle powertrain technologies (BEV vs. PHEV) and uncover market patterns across 5 business dimensions.

🔍 Key Findings:
• Powertrain Dominance: Pure Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) hold 78.3% of the total market, surging to over 82% in recent model years.
• Battery Autonomy Gap: BEVs have a median recorded range of 215 miles compared to 28 miles for PHEVs (a 7.7x autonomy advantage).
• Automaker Landscape: Tesla commands 44.05% market share, followed by Chevrolet and Nissan. While American EV leaders produce exclusively BEVs, legacy European brands (BMW, Volvo) remain heavily reliant on PHEVs.
• Policy Compliance: 100% of vehicles disqualified from CAFV incentives are PHEVs that fail the statutory 30-mile electric range threshold.
• Regional Concentration: King County (Seattle Metro) accounts for 51.9% of all EV registrations in the state.

🤖 Machine Learning Benchmark (5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation):
I implemented, tuned, and benchmarked 5 classification algorithms to predict vehicle powertrain type without post-test leakage:
- 🏆 Gradient Boosting Classifier: 0.9640 ROC-AUC | 90.48% Accuracy | 0.9397 F1
- 🥈 Random Forest Classifier: 0.9626 ROC-AUC | 90.46% Accuracy | 0.9404 F1
- 🥉 Logistic Regression: 0.9465 ROC-AUC | 85.90% Accuracy | 98.67% Precision
- K-Nearest Neighbors & AdaBoost

🛠️ Tech Stack: Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Joblib, Jupyter
